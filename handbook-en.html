SECTION 4

Sport Disciplines, Squad Structure & Competition Rules

Comprehensive regulations for official track, team tournaments, family games, and exhibitions.

4.1 Track Athletics & Team Sports (Student Only — Official Medals)

1. 30-Meter Sprint (09:00 – 09:45)

66 Medals

Quota: 22 runners per house (88 runners total across 4 colors). G1–G5: 2 Boys & 2 Girls per grade; G6: 1 Boy & 1 Girl.

Rules: 22 heats total (11 Boys heats, 11 Girls heats). Direct medal finals across all 22 heats (1st Gold, 2nd Silver, 3rd Bronze). An athlete may not run twice in the same division.

2. 6 × 30m Shuttle Relay (09:45 – 10:00)

72 Medals

Quota: 4 teams × 6 runners = 24 runners per house (96 runners total across 4 colors). 2 runners per grade in team.

Rules: Shuttle relay over 180 meters total. Baton passed within marked zone. Dropped batons must be retrieved by the runner who dropped it. Top 3 teams receive medals for all 6 runners.

3. Football 5v5 (10:00 – 10:15)

15 Medals

Quota: 5 players per team (including 1 goalkeeper). Co-ed allowed. 20 players total.

Rules: Preliminary matches on Mon 4 Jan; Bronze & Championship finals on Event Day. 6 minutes continuous play per match (no halftime). Top 3 teams receive medals for all 5 squad members.

4. Primary Chairball G1–G2 (10:15 – 10:25)

18 Medals

Quota: 6 players per team (5 court players + 1 basket catcher). Co-ed allowed. 24 players total.

Rules: Preliminary matches on Tue 5 Jan; Finals on Courts A & B simultaneously (6 mins continuous). Soft-touch ball. Basket catcher stands safely on the ground without chairs. Top 3 teams receive medals for all 6 squad members (18 medals total).

5. Basketball 3×3 (10:25 – 10:35)

18 Medals

Quota: 6 players per team (3 Junior G3–G4 + 3 Senior G5–G6). Co-ed allowed. 24 players total.

Rules: Preliminary matches on Wed 6 Jan; Finals on Courts A & B simultaneously. Junior squad plays first 3 mins; Senior squad plays final 3 mins. Score runs continuously. Top 3 teams receive medals for all 6 squad members (18 medals total).

4.2 Family Games (Students + Parents — Sweet Snack Treat Awards)

6. Fastball Throwing (10:35 – 10:45)

Quota: 12 students (1 Boy, 1 Girl per grade G1–G6) + 1 parent giant basket holder per house (Total: 48 students, 4 parents).

Rules: 90 seconds rapid throwing. Students throw soft balls from age lines into parent's basket. Most balls caught wins snack hamper.

7. Hula Hoop Relay (10:45 – 10:55)

Quota: 6 students (1 per grade G1–G6) + 6 parents per house (Total: 24 students, 24 parents).

Rules: 90 seconds. Alternating student-parent line hand-in-hand. Pass hoop through body without letting go. Hands must stay linked at all times.

8. 3-Legged Family Relay (10:55 – 11:15)

Quota: 6 pairs per house: Junior Division (G1–G3, 3 pairs) + Senior Division (G4–G6, 3 pairs). Total: 24 pairs.

Rules: Soft quick-release velcro strap. Walking pace only (no sprint leaping). Shuttle relay style where next pair departs only after previous crosses line.

4.3 Special Exhibition Games (Mixed Houses — Joy & Family Bonding)

9. Moms vs Kids Chairball (11:15 – 11:25)

Mother All-Stars (6 Moms: 5 on court + 1 basket catcher) vs Student All-Stars (8 kids + 1 parent basket catcher). 6 minutes match. Soft ball, giant basket on floor, strictly non-contact.

10. Dads vs Kids Giant Soccer (11:25 – 11:35)

Father All-Stars (5 Dads) vs Student All-Stars (7 Kids). 6 minutes match. Oversized soft soccer ball. Speed walking only, no slide tackles, no goalkeepers.

11. Giant Volleyball: Parents vs Teachers (11:35 – 11:50)

Exhibition in two 5-minute halves (10 mins total):
• First 5 mins: Mother All-Stars (6) vs Female Teachers (6)
• Second 5 mins: Father All-Stars (6) vs Male Teachers (6)
Giant lightweight ball. Catch and toss allowed, must pass to at least 1 teammate, 3-second hold limit, no jumping spikes.

SECTION 6

House Master Action Checklist & Safety Protocols

Essential operational responsibilities required of all House Masters and Referees.

1

Athlete Participation Distribution

Ensure every student is rostered to actually compete in at least 1–2 events (combining official sports and Family Games). A 3rd event is only assigned when numbers fall short. Being a bench substitute does not count toward participation.

2

Family Game Parent Confirmation

Verify parent attendance before entering pairs into Family Games. If a parent is unable to attend, re-assign the student to an open event or coordinate a verified proxy guardian well in advance.

3

Roster Conflict Prevention

Verify entries across Color, Grade, Gender, and Schedule times. Specifically ensure no athlete plays both Football and Basketball, and strictly forbid any student running twice in 30m Sprint heats.

4

Advance Parent Notification

Notify parents of their child's confirmed sporting events and official staging call times between December 17–18, 2026, facilitating smooth arrival and awards check-in on event day.

5

Safety Protocols & Referee Briefing

Before each match, referees must demonstrate rules and standard stop-play whistle signals. House Masters must maintain fresh water hydration stations at house bays and coordinate continuously with the first aid nurse station throughout the tournament.
