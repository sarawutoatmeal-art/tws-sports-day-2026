## SECTION 4: SPORT DISCIPLINES, SQUAD STRUCTURE & RULES

### 4.1 Track Athletics & Team Sports (Student Only — Official Medals)
1. **30-Meter Sprint (09:00–09:45):**
   - **Quota:** 22 athletes per house (88 runners total across 4 colors).
   - **Structure:** G1–G5 (2 Boys, 2 Girls per grade); G6 (1 Boy, 1 Girl).
   - **Rules:** 22 heats total (11 Boys heats, 11 Girls heats). Direct medal heats (1st Gold, 2nd Silver, 3rd Bronze). An athlete may not run twice in the same division.
2. **6 × 30m Shuttle Relay (09:45–10:00):**
   - **Quota:** 4 teams × 6 runners = 24 runners per house (96 runners total).
   - **Structure:** 2 runners per grade in team. Total distance = 180 meters.
   - **Rules:** Baton handoff within marked exchange zone. Top 3 teams receive medals for all 6 runners.
3. **Football 5v5 (10:00–10:15):**
   - **Quota:** 5 players per team (including 1 goalkeeper). Total 20 players.
   - **Rules:** 6 minutes continuous, no halftime. Top 3 teams receive medals for all 5 players.
4. **Chairball G1–G2 (10:15–10:25):**
   - **Quota:** 6 players per team (5 court players + 1 basket catcher). Total 24 players.
   - **Rules:** 6 minutes continuous. Soft-touch ball. Basket catcher stands safely on the floor without chairs. Top 3 teams receive medals for all 6 squad members.
5. **Basketball 3×3 (10:25–10:35):**
   - **Quota:** 6 players per team (3 Junior G3–G4 + 3 Senior G5–G6).
   - **Rules:** Junior squad plays first 3 mins; Senior squad plays final 3 mins. Score continues seamlessly. Top 3 teams receive medals for all 6 squad members.

### 4.2 Family Games (Students + Parents — Sweet Snack Treats)
6. **Fastball Throwing (10:35–10:45):**
   - **Quota:** 12 students (1 Boy, 1 Girl per grade G1–G6) + 1 parent catcher per house.
   - **Rules:** 90 seconds rapid throwing. Students throw soft balls from age lines into parent's giant basket.
7. **Hula Hoop Relay (10:45–10:55):**
   - **Quota:** 6 students (1 per grade G1–G6) + 6 parents per house.
   - **Rules:** 90 seconds hand-in-hand human chain. Pass hoop from start to end without breaking hand grip.
8. **3-Legged Family Relay (10:55–11:15):**
   - **Quota:** 6 pairs per house (3 Junior pairs + 3 Senior pairs).
   - **Rules:** Soft velcro quick-release bands. Speed-walking only (no running or leaping) for absolute safety.

### 4.3 Special Exhibition Games (Mixed Kingdom Fun)
9. **Moms vs Kids Chairball (11:15–11:25):** 6 Mothers vs 8 Students. Soft oversized ball, non-contact.
10. **Dads vs Kids Giant Soccer (11:25–11:35):** 5 Fathers vs 7 Students. Giant lightweight soccer ball, speed-walking only, no sliding.
11. **Giant Volleyball: Parents vs Teachers (11:35–11:50):** 12 Parents vs 12 Teachers (5 mins Moms vs Female Teachers; 5 mins Dads vs Male Teachers). Giant inflatable ball, catch-and-pass rule.

## SECTION 6: HOUSE MASTER ESSENTIAL CHECKLIST
1. **Participation Equity:** Every student must be registered for at least 1–2 official events.
2. **Parent Confirmation:** Re-confirm parent availability for Family Games in advance.
3. **No Duplicate Conflict:** Avoid scheduling the same student in conflicting concurrent games (e.g. Football & Basketball).
4. **Parent Advance Notice:** Send event assignments and arrival schedules to parents by Dec 17–18.
5. **Safety First:** Maintain hydration stations, warm-up supervision, and immediate first-aid coordination throughout.
